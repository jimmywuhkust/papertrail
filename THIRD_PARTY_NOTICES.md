# Third-party services and data

PaperTrail uses or links to the following public scholarly infrastructure:

- [OpenAlex](https://openalex.org/) for scholarly works, relationships, and concepts
- [Crossref](https://www.crossref.org/) for DOI metadata
- [IETF Datatracker](https://datatracker.ietf.org/) for RFC and Internet-Draft metadata
- [DOI.org](https://www.doi.org/) for persistent DOI links

The bundled ten-year venue snapshot and demo graph are redistributable metadata snapshots assembled from public scholarly records. Metadata accuracy, author identity disambiguation, and venue classification should be verified against the original publisher record before formal use.

Third-party names and marks belong to their respective owners. Inclusion does not imply endorsement.
